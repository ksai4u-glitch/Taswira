The history map

Start with the continent.

Before there was a Nigeria there was Africa: plates pulling apart, a Sahara that was once green, and cities such as Meroë, Aksum and Great Zimbabwe rising from the map.

Follow the rivers

Down the Niger and the Benue.

Trade, faith and armies moved along these rivers and across the Sahel long before today’s borders were drawn. The map follows them into the land that became Nigeria.

The land that became Nigeria

Cities, caliphates, railways and a civil war.

Play chapters on Nok, the Hausa cities and Kano, Kanem-Bornu, the Sokoto Caliphate, colonial conquest and the railways, independence in 1960 and the Civil War of 1967 to 1970. Every scene shows its sources and what is still disputed.

The history map is a draft. It opens here once a historian has reviewed it.
