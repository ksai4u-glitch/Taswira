    What the map shows

    Each state, and the Federal Capital Territory (FCT), is coloured by the candidate with the most valid votes there. The paler the colour, the closer the race: full colour means the winner had 70% or more. Choose a party under "Colour" to see its share of the vote in every state instead. Party colours are chosen for this map, not the parties' own.

    The national totals are the figures INEC declared. The state figures are the results announced for each state at INEC's national collation centre, as compiled from INEC and press reports (sources below).
