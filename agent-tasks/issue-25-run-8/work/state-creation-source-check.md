## Source 2: Gazette Extraordinary No. 14, Vol. 63, 17 March 1976, Part A

URL: https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/ng/1976/ng-government-gazette-supplement-dated-1976-03-17-no-14-part-a.pdf
(4-page scan, pages A55-A58, 865 KB. I read all four as images.)

| Item | Finding |
|---|---|
| Opened? | Yes. |
| Instrument | **States (Creation and Transitional Provisions) Decree 1976, Decree No. 12.** Dated in the margin 3 February 1976 (commencement; s.7 says it is deemed in force from 3 Feb 1976). "MADE at Lagos this 10th day of March 1976", signed Lt-Gen O. Obasanjo. Gazetted 17 March 1976. The text does not explain the gap between these dates. |
| States | s.6(1) substitutes a new Constitution s.3: "nineteen states": Anambra, Bauchi, Bendel, Benue, Borno, Cross River, Gongola, Imo, Kaduna, Kano, Kwara, Lagos, Niger, Ogun, Ondo, Oyo, Plateau, Rivers, Sokoto. The Schedule lists 19 entries (a)-(s) with area and capital. |
| Areas | Named provinces, divisions, districts and local authority areas, not modern state lines. Examples: Kwara = Ilorin, Igbomina/Ekiti, Lafiagi/Pategi, Kabba, **Kogi**, Igbirra, Oyun and Borgu. Niger = Kontagora, Bida, Minna, Agai/Lapai and **Abuja**. Benue includes "Dekina, Ankpa and Igala Divisions in Kwara State". Several entries cut divisions ("Bauchi Division less Jarawa District", "Opobo Division not included in Cross River"). |
| Boundaries | s.4: the Supreme Military Council may appoint a **States Boundaries Adjustment Commission**; adjustments take effect only after Council approval. So the Schedule lines were explicitly open to later adjustment. |
| FCT | **Not in this decree.** No Federal Capital Territory appears in the Schedule; Abuja is named only as an area within Niger. The "Decree No. 6 of 1976" for the FCT is not in this document. |
| Kogi | Shows only that in 1976 Kogi was a division in Kwara. It says nothing about the 1991 state. |
| Supports | The 19-state count (and the 12 to 19 step, since s.6(3) repeals the 1967 decree and its amendments). s.5 margin note cites "1967 No. 14" as the earlier decree whose continuing law is carried over. |
| Rights | Unknown. The scan says "Published by authority of the Federal Military Government of Nigeria and printed by the Ministry of Information, Printing Division, Lagos". I found no licence. The archive's about page was not readable (HTTP 403 in an earlier run; I did not retry it). |
| Gaps | Transcription is from my reading of page images and needs a copy check. No map or coordinates. Compare against modern ADM1 not done. |

## Effect on the revised Stage 0/1 scope (Claude's assessment on this issue)

- **"States creation 1967-1996 built from geoBoundaries ADM1" (Stage 1, point 2 and section 6 addition): not supported as stated.** The 1967 and 1976 schedules use provinces, divisions and districts, and cut across divisions, so they do not map to modern ADM1 units. Whether a specific schedule area cuts through a modern state was **not checked**, so this is "not demonstrated", not "disproved". The 1987-1996 stages might follow modern lines more closely, but no primary text for those was opened.
- **"Kogi needs LGA-level geometry or an approximate note" (section 6 addition): the "approximate" half is supported** (1976 gazette shows Kogi as a Kwara division; 1991 text missing). The LGA geometry route is not supported by any source seen.
- **"Dated legal snapshots" (Stage 1): supported for 1967 and 1976 as dated legal instruments.** Supported for 1996 only through a speech; not yet for 1987 or 1991.
- **"The FCT should be shown separately from the state counts" (section 3): consistent** with the sources (1976 Decree 12 has no FCT; the 1996 speech says "30 states with an FCT"). The 1976 designation and 1991 move dates rest on secondary snippets.
- **Each feature records evidence type, precision, disputed, source, licence (Stage 0): supported by what I found.** Every source here has unknown rights, and two have no readable page.
- **"Unreviewed draft" label and rights tracking: reinforced.** No source gives a reuse licence. Gazettes say only "Published by authority of the Federal Military Government"; archivi.ng says all rights reserved.
- **Not tested by these sources:** the Nok chapter, accessibility, time model, build script, user testing, deep time.
