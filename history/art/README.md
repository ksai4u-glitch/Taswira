# Original Taswira visual studies

Saifu selected the hare direction on 27 October 2026 after reviewing six original concepts.
Zomo is the working name for Taswira's fictional guide; it is Hausa for hare and has
documented storytelling associations. This character is an original editorial guide,
not a historical eyewitness or a reproduction of a modern folktale-book character.

`zomo-poses.png`: AI-assisted original illustration, directed for Taswira in this session.
1536×1024 transparent PNG, four columns/two rows. Poses 0–3: idle, present-left,
present-right, reading; 4–7: crouch, discovery, listening, solemn. The live prototype
omits pose 4 because it crosses its cell boundary. Pose 5 uses a CSS inset to exclude
spill from that adjacent pose. A production vector/puppet rig and revised walking
pose remain work for the next animation pass. Do not claim eight finished animation states.

Motion, entrances, pose choice and stillness are derived from scene time. During
sensitive scenes the guide uses a still solemn pose, with no comic reaction or bouncing.
No voice recording has been produced; current narration is captioned text.

`arewa-cultural-study.svg`: original geometric interlace exploration based on the
documented design vocabulary discussed in the accompanying research. It is not a
tracing of the supplied raster variants, a claim of one correct traditional pattern,
or a dated historical flag/seal. It is deliberately separate from actor emblems.

The concept-board PNG in previews is original art direction. Its animal personalities
are proposed character design, not traditional meanings; the hornbill is not a
verified species model or a claim of Hausa hornbill mythology.
