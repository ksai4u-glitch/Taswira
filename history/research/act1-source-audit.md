## Corrections that matter to the visual design

1. **Urbanism is not synonymous with monarchy.** The Met's interpretation of Djenné-Djenno
   describes self-governance and no identified palace/temple hierarchy. Give archaeology its
   own visual form rather than inventing a ruler's seat for every stop.
2. **Architecture must appear in its documented period.** Aksum's stelae and Great Zimbabwe's
   enclosure support different architectural dates from the beginning of their settlements.
3. **Capital location is evidence, not decoration.** The current Frobenius report updates the
   older blanket statement that Njimi is entirely unlocated: Tié is now a proposed identification.
   Keep that identification qualified rather than replacing one overstatement with another.
4. **An overview needs explicit rewinds.** Act 0 ends thousands of years ago, but this act opens
   around 300,000 years ago. The later Africa tour also reaches medieval times before returning
   to the much earlier Nok chapter. Both reversals need authored narration and on-screen date
   cues; they must not imply a continuous chronological progression or a migration genealogy.

- UNESCO General History of Africa volumes have **not** been read for this audit. They remain
  a proposed cross-check, not an approved or completed second source for every stop.
- Niger length, source-to-mouth route, the Benue confluence and any animated trade links need
  primary geographic/route checks. Search surfaced a World Bank document, but it was not opened
  and is not treated as verified. Do not draw a source-to-mouth route from a convenient handful
  of city coordinates.
- All exact coordinates, capital identifications, dates for monument silhouettes and the
  final narration need a scene/feature source record before being wired into playback.

## Engine work needed after the source gates

The current non-globe map checks constrain cameras to latitude 2–20, longitude 0–18 and zoom 4–11.
Act 1 needs a named African map area with separately validated camera bounds and overview zoom,
without weakening the checks for existing Nigeria chapters. The background already covers Africa
at coarser resolution, according to `history/geo/README.md`; this is not proof of period coastlines.

Required implementation checks:

- The Africa overview includes southern Africa and the East African coast, with no modern tiles.
- Year-ago and BCE/CE labels have an explicit transition; rewinds are readable in live and video modes.
- Site, self-governing town and ruler's seat are distinct; no filled empire or ethnic boundary.
- A monument never appears before its documented construction interval.
- Keep the five zoom stages: Africa → sub-Saharan Africa → West Africa → lower Niger/Benue →
  the region that later became Nigeria. Final frame has no national outline.
- Re-run normal, reduced-motion, small-screen and deterministic video tests after adding the act.
