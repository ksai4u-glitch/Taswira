## Integration tests rejected before delivery

The existing source mounts and their shadows are baked into the plate. Several native tests failed visual review:

- An assumed-ground-plane temporal fill left conspicuous road patches. A separate triangulated plane attempt was inconsistent and was rejected as a ground calibration.
- Local material healing stayed visibly soft; automatic adjacent-texture cloning sometimes selected architecture as a donor. Source-plane tracking improved registration but did not produce a clean enough plate.
- Broad ground resurfacing removed too much of the original road texture, architectural lighting and lived detail.
- Replacing the source mounts in place required silhouette/hoof registration and still looked poorly integrated in inspected frames. Those tests are not approved replacements.

None of these tests was uploaded as a new finished film. The original licensed asset, native scenes, source/mask guides, rejected trials and work ledger remain private production files. No model binary is redistributed. Local segmentation/detection utilities were used to analyse the owner’s frames, not to train on the licensed horse model.
