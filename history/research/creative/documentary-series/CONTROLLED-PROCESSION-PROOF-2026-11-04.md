## Production method

The horse is one fixed textured mesh with a 39-bone rig and an authored walk cycle, not independently generated anatomy on successive frames. Native rider meshes follow the saddle; reins follow the hands and mouth attachment. Walk phases differ between the two actors. Sampled stance travel is matched at approximately 1.34 m/s; foot-height correction compensates small skin-surface penetration. These are setup measurements, not a claim that a biomechanical or artistic review is complete.

A static-background reconstruction supplies a preliminary camera fit. The ground orientation and scale are approximate integration choices, not surveyed Kano geometry. The gate needs separate facade and rear-parapet holdouts: an internal facade-only trial put a rider on the roof and was rejected. Dense source inspection corrected the holdout as the gate passed out of view. The full-shot repair will require more complete masks and plate cleanup than this two-second study.

The city layer is rendered at 1080×1920; the diagnostic panel is rendered at 960×960 within the portrait canvas. Blender/Cycles, OpenCV, FFmpeg, NumPy/SciPy and Pillow perform the work. Native controls and current assembly code are retained with the production files. No paid image/video/speech/music job was submitted for the study.
