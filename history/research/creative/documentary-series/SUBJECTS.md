## Decision

Proceed with **Sokoto: The Burden of Reform, 1804–1817** as the next research commission after the Kano foundation. It offers the strongest combination of a bounded dramatic transformation, substantial accessible scholarship, a direct bridge from Kano and a reusable geographic framework. This is a production-readiness decision, not a ranking of historical importance.

The current stage has already begun: [SOKOTO.md](SOKOTO.md) contains an original opening draft, claim-level source annotations, a five-part structure and acquisition priorities. No new episode imagery or voice has been generated. Nana Asma'u's mature educational work belongs in a later c.1830–1865 study, with its date basis checked; she was a child during the 1804 departure. The conquest of 1903 and the prolonged transformation of slavery can follow without pretending the whole nineteenth century fits one film.

Treat this as a sequence of commissions, not a fixed season order or a claim that every source is cleared. After Kano, reassess the next subject using actual research access and production results. Keep the Kano story and separate Taswira teaser ahead of new-episode asset spending.
