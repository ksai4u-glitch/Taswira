## Where the previous handling failed

The technical checks were real: the file decodes, browser playback completed, and the accepted audio waveform was preserved. Those checks address delivery and synchronization. They do not answer whether the horsemen look alive, grounded and convincing.

The native repair was a partial edge treatment presented as a satisfactory response to a broader motion-quality complaint. The acceptance test was misdirected. The responsibility is the production review, not the owner’s instructions. The next attempt must start from this failed visual requirement, not from another smoothing metric.

The retained difference-matte code uses colour differences, morphology and a moving road corridor rather than semantic horse/rider segmentation. That method can vary coverage when foreground colours resemble the plate; it has no anatomical model. The later motion-guided alpha averaging cannot repair unconvincing anatomy already present in its source. This explains the limit of the attempted repair; it does not establish a measured cause for every individual leg defect.
