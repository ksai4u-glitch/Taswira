## Lessons to retain

| Problem observed | Change to the production process |
|---|---|
| A push towards a gate read as a static zoom or stopped before entering | Prove forward translation, parallax and the endpoint in a native camera guide; specify where the camera finishes relative to the gate and city. |
| A small courtyard failed to read as a kingdom | Establish a large continuous urban area with a clear avenue, wall/house scale and distant skyline before descending. |
| Gates risked becoming identical or uniformly oversized | Research named gates separately; vary forms/heights where justified, and mark art-directed scale as illustrative. |
| Generated ascent/mid-shot architecture melted or ghosted | Inspect dense samples and playback around the transition. Reject bad intervals; control formation/topology in native animation or use a motivated cut. |
| Short-generation economy encouraged too much action in one shot | Give each shot one clear purpose and a stable end frame. Do not buy another attempt until its failure mode and required repair are identified. |
| Narration became a string of poetic questions | Build a concrete claim/causality spine, then use a few questions and silences for dramatic shape. |
| City grandeur obscured the people and institutions behind it | Include documented commerce, religion, captivity, coercion and everyday life; do not mistake anonymous crowd imagery for social history. |
| Frame counts or speech recognition could be mistaken for quality approval | Verify full browser playback, visual continuity, date/label readability, listening and historical evidence separately. |
