    What the colours mean

    Each colour is the European power that claimed the land on that date, as recorded by CShapes 2.0. A claim on paper often came years before real control: Britain claimed northern Nigeria from 1900, but the Sokoto Caliphate fell only in 1903. Dark land is land no European power claimed yet, ruled by African states and peoples.

    The share is the area of land claimed by Britain, France, Germany, Belgium (King Leopold's Congo), Portugal, Italy and Spain, out of all the land of Africa and its islands mapped by CShapes on 1 January 1914.
